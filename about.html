Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began when a small group of neighbors started fostering animals out of their own homes. Today we partner with local shelters, veterinarians, and hundreds of volunteers to rescue and rehome animals across the metro area every year.

Core Values

  • Compassion for every animal, regardless of age or medical need
  • Transparency with our donors and community
  • Education to help prevent animal homelessness

Partner Acknowledgments

We are grateful to our partner veterinary clinics, local pet supply stores, and community sponsors whose ongoing support helps us care for every animal that comes through our doors.
